Meet Fran

The Sweet Soul Behind Every Cookie

From healthcare professional to cookie artist, Fran's journey is one of passion, creativity, and an unwavering commitment to spreading joy through incredible cookies.

Fran's Story

Frances discovered her passion for baking during a challenging period in her life. What started as a therapeutic hobby quickly blossomed into something extraordinary. Her background in healthcare taught her the importance of precision, care, and attention to detail - qualities that now define every cookie she creates.

After years of perfecting her craft and sharing her creations with friends and family, Fran realized she had something special. Her cookies weren't just desserts; they were experiences that brought people together and created lasting memories.

Today, FROBANDYCAKES COOKEZ represents Fran's commitment to excellence and her belief that every cookie should be a moment of pure joy. Each creation is stuffed with not just incredible ingredients, but with love, passion, and a little bit of magic.
