Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our cookie magic!

How do I place an order?

Simply follow us on Instagram and send us a DM with your cookie preferences! We'll take care of the rest.

How much do cookies weigh?

Our cookies are substantial! Each one weighs between 5.5-7 oz - that's a lot of deliciousness in every bite!

Are the cookie flavors always the same?

Nope! Each cookie is uniquely crafted. While we have signature styles, every cookie is a little different - that's part of the magic!

How far in advance should I order?

We recommend ordering at least 24-48 hours in advance to ensure we can craft your perfect cookies with love and care!
